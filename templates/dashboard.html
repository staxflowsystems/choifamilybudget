❄️ The Debt Snowball - How to Win
Dave Ramsey's proven method: Pay smallest debts first to build momentum. Become debt-free. Then invest and build wealth.
💡 How It Works

1. List debts: Smallest balance to largest
2. Pay minimums: On everything
3. Attack smallest: Put all extra money here
4. Snowball effect: When paid off, roll payment to next
5. Repeat: Until all debt is gone

🎯 Why Smallest First?

"Personal finance is 80% behavior, 20% math." — Dave Ramsey

Paying the smallest debt first gives you a quick WIN. You see progress fast. That feeling motivates you to keep going. It's psychological, not mathematical. Like a snowball rolling downhill — it starts small but grows bigger and faster with every debt you eliminate.

🚫 Why You Must Eliminate Debt

Debt steals your future. Every dollar in interest payments is money that should go toward YOUR dreams — a home, retirement, security.

You work hard. Your money should work for you, not the bank.

✅ After Debt: The Path to Wealth

Once debt-free, take that monthly payment and invest it. $675/month in VOO for 15 years = $250,000+. That's real wealth. That's financial freedom.

🎉 Your Debt Free Plan

📋 Dave Ramsey's Baby Steps After Debt
Once your debts are paid off, take the money you were throwing at debt and redirect it into savings and investing. Here's the order Dave Ramsey recommends.
🚨
Baby Step 1: $1,000 Emergency Fund
Before attacking debt, save $1,000 in a separate savings account. This is your safety net for small surprises — flat tire, small medical bill. Don't touch it except for real emergencies.
Goal: Save $1,000 first. No exceptions.
💳
Baby Step 2: Pay Off All Debt (Snowball)
Use the Snowball tab to pay off every debt from smallest to largest. Put every extra dollar toward debt while paying minimums on everything else.
This is where you are now. Focus here!
🛡️
Baby Step 3: Full Emergency Fund (3-6 Months)
Once debt-free, build your emergency fund to cover 3-6 months of living expenses. If monthly expenses are $4,500, your goal is $13,500-$27,000 saved.
This protects you if Dad loses income or has a health issue.
📈
Baby Step 4: Invest 15% of Income
Put 15% of your monthly income into retirement investments. With $4,500/month income, that's $675/month. Use VOO in a brokerage account, or a 401k/IRA if available.
$675/month in VOO for 15 years could grow to $250,000+
🏠
Baby Step 5+: Build Wealth
Keep investing, pay off your home if you have one, and build wealth. At this stage you have no debt, an emergency fund, and growing investments. You are financially free.
This is the goal. Freedom from financial stress.
